A NOTE ABOUT THE EXPERIENCE

One story.
Many discoveries.

This is a guided visual introduction, not an encyclopedia. Choose a glowing point to explore a milestone; the story card updates instantly. Use the arrow controls or press ← and → on your keyboard to move through history.

The three paths are conceptual: they connect the evolution of algorithms, computing power, and data. Their heights are not quantitative measurements, and later decades are intentionally given extra horizontal space so you can explore them.
